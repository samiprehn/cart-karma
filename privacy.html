🛒 Cart Karma — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This policy covers the Cart Karma browser extension and the Cart Karma website. The short version: Cart Karma does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal data.

What the extension does

On the search pages of supported retailers, Cart Karma reads the search term from the page's URL and displays a small card with links to the same search on independent and secondhand marketplaces (Etsy, eBay, Craigslist, OfferUp, and Facebook Marketplace). The search term is used only on your device to build those links — it is never stored or sent anywhere.

Data we collect

None. Cart Karma has no servers, no analytics, and makes no network requests of its own. We do not collect personally identifiable information, browsing history, location, or any other user data.

Local settings

The extension saves one setting — your preferred Craigslist region (for example, "sandiego") — using your browser's local storage. This value stays on your device, is used only to point the Craigslist link at your area, and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Permissions

  • Storage: to save your Craigslist region setting locally.
  • Host access to specific retailer search pages: to read the search term and show the card on those pages.

Third parties

Cart Karma does not sell or transfer user data to third parties. The marketplace links simply open standard search URLs in a new tab; once you follow a link, that destination site's own privacy policy applies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will reflect it.
